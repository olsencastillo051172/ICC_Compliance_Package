## 📖 Overview
ICC (IA Compliance Core) is a turnkey SaaS compliance and trust layer designed for cloud‑native environments. It provides reproducible evidence through CI/CD pipelines, secrets rotation, and auditable runbooks. ICC integrates seamlessly with Kubernetes (GKE, EKS, AKS), supports multi‑cloud deployment, and includes bilingual documentation for onboarding auditors, buyers, and investors.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Publication in Google Cloud Marketplace with complete compliance documentation.  
- Reproducible evidence: CI/CD, network policies, secrets rotation, audit trails.  
- Bilingual runbooks and guides for incident response and uninstallation.  
- Extended descriptions tailored for auditors, investors, and clients.  

---

## 📂 Package Structure
ICC_Compliance_Package/  
├── listing-summary.md  
│   Executive summary for marketplaces and auditors. Describes ICC, its value proposition, compliance coverage, and subscription tiers (Starter, Pro, Enterprise + White‑Label).  
│  
├── buyer-pack.md  
│   Deployment and operations guide for enterprise buyers. Includes step‑by‑step instructions (Helm/Terraform), infrastructure cost breakdowns, operations manual, and buyer checklists.  
│  
├── pitch-deck.md  
│   15‑slide presentation for investors and partners. Covers problem, opportunity, solution, demo, business model, roadmap, and call to action.  
│  
├── srm-incident.md  
│   Incident Response Runbook (Spanish & English). Outlines detection, classification, containment, mitigation, communication, recovery, and post‑mortem steps.  
│  
└── ICC_Uninstallation_Runbook.md  
    Step‑by‑step uninstall procedure (bilingual). Details Helm uninstall, Terraform destroy, secrets cleanup, and verification of resource removal.
