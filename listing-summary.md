# IA Compliance Core (ICC)

**IA Compliance Core (ICC)** is an enterprise-grade AI compliance enablement platform designed for cloud-native environments. ICC helps organizations operationalize AI governance through system inventory, risk mapping, incident workflows, and audit-ready evidence generation—without providing legal advice or issuing regulatory certifications.

ICC is built for regulated and compliance-driven organizations that require **audit readiness by design**, reproducible deployments, and strong security posture across Kubernetes-based infrastructures. The platform supports multi-tenant and multi-cloud deployments (GKE, EKS, AKS) and is packaged with documented runbooks and evidence indexes suitable for internal audits and external assessments.

### Key Capabilities
- AI system inventory and traceability
- Risk classification and governance workflows
- Incident management and corrective actions
- Immutable evidence logs and exportable audit reports (PDF/XLS)
- Enterprise-grade security controls (RBAC, 2FA support, private networking)
- Marketplace-ready deployment with reproducible infrastructure

### Compliance Enablement Scope
ICC supports **readiness and control mapping** for major frameworks, including:
- EU AI Act (governance artifacts and evidence mapping)
- NIST AI RMF
- ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System)
- ISO/IEC 27001 / 27701
- SOC 2 readiness
- GDPR / LGPD / CCPA (privacy workflows)

> ICC enables compliance operations and evidence workflows. It does **not** provide legal advice, certification, or regulator authority.
