# Incident Response Runbook (SRM)
## IA Compliance Core (ICC)

**Product:** IA Compliance Core (ICC)  
**Publisher:** (IO) Olsen Intelligence  
**Scope:** Public operational runbook for marketplace review, auditors, and enterprise buyers  
**Version:** v1.0.0  

---

## 1. Purpose
This Incident Response Runbook defines the standardized process used by ICC to detect, assess, respond to, and document security or operational incidents.

This document is intended for:
- Enterprise customers
- Auditors and compliance reviewers
- Google Cloud Marketplace reviewers

ICC provides **incident response enablement and documentation workflows**.  
It does **not** replace customer-specific SOCs, legal teams, or regulatory authorities.

---

## 2. Incident Definition
An incident is any event that:
- Impacts confidentiality, integrity, or availability of ICC services
- Affects audit evidence integrity
- Causes unauthorized access or data exposure
- Results in service disruption or security policy violation

---

## 3. Incident Classification
Incidents are classified into the following severity levels:

| Severity | Description |
|--------|------------|
| Critical | Confirmed data breach, evidence tampering, or prolonged service outage |
| High | Unauthorized access attempt, partial service degradation |
| Medium | Policy violation, misconfiguration without data exposure |
| Low | Minor operational issue, no security impact |

---

## 4. Detection & Reporting
Incidents may be detected through:
- Monitoring and alerting systems
- Audit log review
- Customer or partner reports
- Internal operational checks

**Reporting channel:**  
Email: **olsencastillo@io-io.net**

Sensitive details should be minimized and shared responsibly.

---

## 5. Response Workflow
### Step 1 – Identification
- Confirm incident validity
- Assign severity level
- Log initial findings

### Step 2 – Containment
- Isolate affected components
- Prevent further impact
- Preserve logs and evidence

### Step 3 – Analysis
- Determine root cause
- Assess scope and impact
- Identify affected assets

### Step 4 – Mitigation & Recovery
- Apply corrective actions
- Restore normal operations
- Validate system integrity

### Step 5 – Documentation
- Record timeline and actions taken
- Preserve evidence artifacts
- Update internal incident registry

---

## 6. Communication
- Customers are notified when incidents materially affect service or evidence integrity
- Communications are factual, non-speculative, and timely
- Regulatory notification remains customer responsibility unless contractually agreed

---

## 7. Post-Incident Review
After resolution:
- Conduct internal review
- Identify preventive improvements
- Update documentation if required

---

## 8. Evidence & Audit Trail
For each incident, ICC maintains:
- Incident summary
- Timeline of actions
- Affected components
- Resolution status

These records support **audit readiness**, not regulatory certification.

---

## 9. Disclaimer
ICC provides incident response enablement and documentation workflows.  
It does **not** replace customer SOCs, legal counsel, or regulatory authorities.  
Final compliance and notification decisions remain **customer-owned and human-approved**.
